Nervous System

The nervous system is a complex network of nerves and cells that carry messages to and from the brain and spinal cord to various parts of the body. It is responsible for controlling and coordinating all bodily functions, including movement, sensation, thought, and emotion. The nervous system is divided into two main parts: the central nervous system (CNS), which consists of the brain and spinal cord, and the peripheral nervous system (PNS), which includes all the nerves that branch out from the CNS to the rest of the body.
